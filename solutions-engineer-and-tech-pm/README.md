# Solutions Engineer & Tech PM Convergence

The convergence material for Ch 42 §42.5: how Solutions Engineer (SE) and Technical Product Manager (TPM) roles are absorbing AI-tooling fluency as a core requirement, and what that means for hiring, leveling, and the question of whether these stay separate roles.

## The book's stance

> Two roles that were drifting apart pre-AI are now converging:
>
> - **Solutions Engineers** (in customer-facing teams) increasingly write working code in customer environments. The line between SE and engineer blurs. They need real engineering skills, not just product knowledge.
> - **Tech PMs** increasingly write executable specs (Chapter 4), workshop them with the Intake pattern (Chapter 19), and ship features with light engineering involvement on bounded T1/T2 work. The line between PM and engineer blurs in the other direction.
>
> The implication: these roles are becoming part of the engineering competency ladder, not adjacent to it. Hire and compensate accordingly.
>
> — Ch 42 §42.5

The artifacts here are the operational implementation: updated JDs, new interview signals, the 2027 question of "when does this stop being a separate role."

## What this is NOT

- **Not a recommendation to merge SE and engineering today.** The convergence is in motion; the merge is not. See [`when-this-stops-being-a-separate-role.md`](when-this-stops-being-a-separate-role.md) for the signals.
- **Not for non-technical PMs.** This material assumes the TPM is technical to begin with. Non-technical PMs working with engineering have a different problem set.
- **Not a sales-engineering replacement.** SE work has a customer-relationship dimension that the convergence does not eliminate. Engineers who can also sell are rarer than SEs who can also engineer.
- **Not for support engineers or technical account managers.** Those roles have different convergence dynamics.

## What this WILL do

- Update your JDs to reflect 2026 hiring reality
- Sharpen interview signals to filter for the new core competencies
- Calibrate comp expectations for hires who clear the new bar
- Surface the 2027 organizational question (do we keep separate roles?) early enough to plan

## What this will NOT do

- Will not turn a non-technical PM or pre-engineering SE into the new bar overnight. The convergence requires real engineering investment from people who started in adjacent roles.
- Will not work in companies where engineering treats SE/TPM as second-class. Cultural respect matters more than the JD update.
- Will not eliminate the need for sales engineers who deeply understand customer use cases vs. engineers who deeply understand the system. Both still matter.
