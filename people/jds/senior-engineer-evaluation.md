## About the role

[REPLACE — 2-3 sentences]

We're hiring a Senior Engineer with depth in **Evaluation**: the feedback loop that lets the system learn whether it's delivering the experience Direction defined, within the constraints Architecture set. A mature Evaluation function does two things the immature one doesn't: it triages, and it moves fast.

This is the role that in many teams has historically been split across QA, SRE, and customer support, and that AI-native engineering organizations are increasingly making explicit and senior.

## What you'll do [KEEP]

- Build and own the team's evaluation infrastructure: regression test suites, model evals, customer-experience monitoring, SLO dashboards, the failed-one-shot triage practice.
- Run the quarterly evaluation cycles: model performance benchmarks, prompt-injection test suite, A/B framework integration, customer-experience review.
- Triage signals coming in from production. Distinguish (a) bugs that violate expectations, (b) quality issues that could be improved but aren't broken, and (c) feature requests that expand scope. Each gets a different response, and conflating them is how engineering teams burn out.
- Partner with Customer Success on the customer-feedback-to-engineering pipeline. Build the tooling that makes "the customer reported X" actionable rather than overwhelming.
- Lead the team's incident response and postmortem practice. Use AI tooling to accelerate (Ch 39); use judgment to decide what changes.

## What we're looking for [KEEP]

**Required:** Senior IC AI-native baseline PLUS:

- Demonstrated experience building evaluation infrastructure: test harnesses, eval frameworks, monitoring, triage processes.
- Comfort with the statistics of evaluation: power analysis, multiple-comparison correction, the difference between "we measured this" and "we measured this in a way that catches regressions."
- Strong customer-empathy muscle. The Evaluation discipline only works if you can hear what the user is actually saying through the noise of the channel.
- A concrete worked example of an evaluation system you built that caught a real regression.

**Preferred:**

- Background in QA engineering, SRE, observability tooling, or customer-experience analytics.
- Experience with model evaluations specifically — benchmark design, prompt sensitivity analysis, eval-as-code patterns.

## Interview process [KEEP]

1. Intro call.
2. **Evaluation design exercise** (90 minutes async): given a (real, anonymized) feature, design the evaluation that would tell us within 14 days whether it's working.
3. **Triage simulation** (60 minutes): we share a week's worth of customer feedback (anonymized), monitoring alerts, and PR comments; you triage them in real time.
4. PR review exercise.
5. Team interviews.
6. References.

## What we won't do [KEEP]

- We won't expect you to be a research scientist. The Evaluation role is engineering, not academic ML.
