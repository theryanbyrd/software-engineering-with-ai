# Career Ladder Additions

Additions to your existing IC and manager career ladders to recognize the new AI-native engineering signals. From Chapter 60 §60.1 of [_Software Engineering with AI_](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ryanbyrd) by Ryan Byrd.

## What's in here

- [`ic-track-additions.md`](ic-track-additions.md) — IC L3-L7 additions: harness contribution, code review judgment, depth in Direction / Architecture / Evaluation
- [`manager-track-additions.md`](manager-track-additions.md) — M1-M3 additions: AI-tooling fluency, the new performance review structure, retention plays

## How to use these

These are **additions** to whatever ladder you already have. They do not replace your existing rubric. Read them with your current ladder document open and identify which items are net-new versus modifications of existing criteria.

## What we explicitly DO NOT do

- **No separate "AI engineer" ladder.** Per Ch 60, the signals integrate into the existing IC track. Vendors selling you a separate AI-engineer career path tend to ghettoize the AI-fluent engineers and create a two-tier system.
- **No comp band recommendations.** Comp bands depend on geography, market, and company stage. Ch 60 §60.2 recommends a 10-20% premium at the senior tier; the actual numbers belong in your comp committee.

## Calibration cadence

The handbook recommends quarterly review of these additions during the rollout phase, easing to annual once the rubrics are settled. AI tooling moves fast; the criteria that worked in Q1 2026 may need adjustment by Q4.
