# Performance Review Additions

Three new sections to add to your existing performance review template, per Chapter 60 §60.3 of [_Software Engineering with AI_](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ryanbyrd) by Ryan Byrd.

## What's in here

- [`harness-contribution.md`](harness-contribution.md) — what did the engineer ship that improved the team's AI-native experience?
- [`ai-authorship-attribution.md`](ai-authorship-attribution.md) — reflection prompt: how does the engineer think about and disclose AI authorship?
- [`review-discipline.md`](review-discipline.md) — assessment of whether the engineer finds the seven slop signatures and reviews thoroughly.

## How to use

These are **additions** to your existing performance review template, not replacements. Add the three sections; do not delete the rest of your template.

The handbook explicitly notes: "These are not radical changes. They are extensions of the existing performance categories" (Ch 60 §60.3).

## Cadence

Add these to the **annual review** (or your equivalent biannual). For shorter check-ins (quarterly 1:1s, mid-year), the harness-contribution and review-discipline questions are useful as conversation prompts; the AI-authorship-attribution reflection is heavier and probably belongs in the annual cycle.

## What this is NOT

- **Not a separate AI-engineering performance track.** Same reasoning as the career ladder: integrate into existing reviews rather than create a parallel structure.
- **Not a performance-improvement-plan template.** PIPs have their own legal and HR considerations; consult your HR/legal team.
- **Not a replacement for self-assessment.** These sections augment the engineer's self-assessment; they don't replace it.

## Calibration tip

When you first roll out these sections, run them on yourself or your senior staff first. The questions surface gaps the engineer didn't realize they had; the manager wants to be calibrated on what answers look like at each level before grading.
