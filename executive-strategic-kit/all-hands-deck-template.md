## Slide 2 — Our posture (02 / 07)

**Headline:** Investment, not substitution.

We are investing in AI tooling **on top of our team** — not as a substitute for it.

What this means in practice:

1. **We're funding tools and harness.** Per-seat licenses, the platform team's harness work, training time. Real budget, not vibes.
2. **We're measuring honestly.** Lead time, change failure rate, cost. We share the dashboard. We renegotiate if the numbers don't track.
3. **We're investing in your skills.** Training, certification, paid time to learn. Engineers who skill up here will be more marketable, not less.

---

## Slide 3 — What we are NOT doing (03 / 07)

Three things to be clear on, in writing:

| Commitment | Detail |
|---|---|
| **NO LAYOFFS tied to the AI rollout.** | We are not planning headcount reductions justified by AI productivity. If business conditions change separately, we will tell you separately and not blame the technology. |
| **NO QUOTAS on AI usage.** | We are not measuring you on number of AI tool invocations, percentage of code AI-authored, or any other "AI usage" metric. The metrics we care about are unchanged — quality, throughput, customer outcomes. |
| **NO MANDATE on adoption.** | You don't have to use AI tooling. The pilot team is opt-in. We expect adoption to grow as people see what works — but it grows through demonstration, not directive. |

## Slide 4 — How rollout works (04 / 07)

Pilot first, harden the harness, then expand carefully.

| Weeks | Phase | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | **Pilot** | [Pilot team] runs through the harness; we measure failures and gaps. |
| 5–8 | **Harden** | Build skills, hooks, subagents. Pilot team's failures are the spec. |
| 9–12 | **Expand** | Add team 2 (and team 3 if pace allows). Honesty checkpoint at Day 60. |
| 13+ | **Steady state** | Quarterly reviews, harness investments, new team onboarding cadence. |

**Key principle:** We expand only as fast as the harness can support. Going faster than the platform team can support is how AI rollouts fail. We are not in a hurry to fail.

## Slide 5 — What's in it for you (05 / 07)

Three concrete commitments to engineers, not platitudes.

### Career

- Re-leveled rubric: harness contributions count for promotion
- Performance reviews extend; they don't replace what already counts
- Internal mobility — choose what you work on within reason

### Compensation

- Senior tier (L4–L5) gets a market-aligned premium for AI fluency
- Comp bands updated annually based on market data
- No comp clawback if you leave the AI work for traditional work

### Support

- Paid learning time — 4 hours/week for the first quarter
- Pairing with senior engineers on review and harness work
- External training budget [$XK/engineer/year]

## Slide 6 — How to get involved (06 / 07)

### Any engineer — Try the harness

1. Read CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md in your repo
2. Run `verify` and confirm it passes
3. Try one of the starter skills on a real task
4. Share what worked and what didn't in #ai-eng

### Engineers with ideas — Contribute to the harness

1. Propose a skill, hook, or subagent in #ai-eng
2. Open a PR with the implementation
3. Get feedback from platform team
4. Ship it; it ends up in our company plugin

### Engineers who love tooling — Join the platform team

1. Talk to [Platform Lead] about the work
